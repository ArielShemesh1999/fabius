# screenshot

## What it does

Capture desktop, app windows, or pixel regions across macOS, Windows, and Linux. Use for marketing screenshots, design reviews, and bug reports.

## When to use

Invoke when the user needs a captured image of: a full desktop, a specific app window, a pixel-coordinate region, or a timed/triggered capture.

## How to use

Ask the agent to invoke this skill by name (`screenshot`) or with one of the trigger phrases in the frontmatter.

The skill handles:
- Full-screen or single-window capture
- Pixel-region selection by coordinates
- Timed or event-triggered capture
- Output as PNG or JPEG with optional scaling
