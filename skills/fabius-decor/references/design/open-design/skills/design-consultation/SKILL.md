# design-consultation

## What it does

Build a complete design system from scratch with creative risks and realistic product mockups. Use for kickoff workshops and brand-from-zero work.

## When to use

- Starting a new product or brand with no existing visual identity.
- Running a design kickoff where the team needs a concrete direction, not just options.
- Generating realistic mockups alongside a design system so decisions are grounded in real UI.

## How to invoke

Ask the agent by name (`design-consultation`) or with one of the trigger phrases in this skill's frontmatter.

The agent will:
1. Ask for brand context: product name, audience, competitors, emotional target.
2. Propose 2–3 distinct creative directions with rationale for each risk taken.
3. Build out the chosen direction into a full DESIGN.md and representative mockups.
