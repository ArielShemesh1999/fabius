# sora

Generate, remix, and manage short video clips via OpenAI's Sora API. Use for cinematic shots, b-roll, and rapid concept video iteration.

## When to use

Invoke when a user needs AI-generated video footage: establishing shots, product b-roll, abstract concept clips, or rapid visual drafts before committing to full production.

## How to use

1. Describe the desired clip: subject, camera motion, lighting, mood, duration.
2. Call the Sora API with your prompt; iterate on style parameters as needed.
3. Review the generated clip and remix (vary, extend, or blend) until it fits the edit.
4. Export and integrate into the target video project.

Trigger this skill by name (`sora`) or with one of the trigger phrases in the frontmatter.
