# creative-director

## What it does

Acts as an experienced creative director through a structured 5-phase process:

1. **Brief deconstruction** — surfaces the real problem behind the ask
2. **Ideation** — applies 20+ methodologies (SIT, TRIZ, Bisociation, SCAMPER, Synectics, and more)
3. **Concept development** — expands the strongest ideas into full creative platforms
4. **Self-assessment** — scores each concept on 3 axes calibrated against Cannes Lions, D&AD, and HumanKind award criteria
5. **Presentation** — delivers a crisp creative rationale ready to pitch

## When to use

- Campaign concepting from a brief
- Critiquing and elevating existing creative work
- Pressure-testing a concept against award-level standards
- Generating divergent ideas with structured methodology

## How to use

Provide the brief (product, audience, objective, tone, any mandatories). Invoke by name (`creative-director`) or with a trigger phrase from the frontmatter.

Output: multiple developed concepts with methodology tags, 3-axis scoring, and a recommended direction with presentation framing.
