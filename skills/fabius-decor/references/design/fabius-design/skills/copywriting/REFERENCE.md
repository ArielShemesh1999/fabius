# copywriting

## What it does

Writes and rewrites marketing copy at a senior level: landing pages, homepages, product ads, email subject lines, and CTAs. Operates as a copy chief partner — it pushes back on weak hooks, sharpens value propositions, and matches voice to audience.

## When to use

- First draft of a landing page or homepage
- Rewriting existing copy that isn't converting
- Launch prep where you need a fast, high-quality copy review
- Ad copy across platforms (Meta, Google, LinkedIn)

## How to use

Provide the product/service, target audience, and goal. Optionally share existing copy to rewrite. Invoke by name (`copywriting`) or with a trigger phrase from the frontmatter.

Output: structured copy ready to paste — headline, subhead, body, and CTA — with brief rationale on strategic choices.
