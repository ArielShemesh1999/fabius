# marketing-psychology

## What it does

Apply psychological principles and behavioral science to copy and design. Useful for tightening hooks, framing, and pricing presentation.

## When to use

- Writing or auditing conversion copy (headlines, CTAs, pricing)
- Diagnosing low-performing landing pages or onboarding flows
- Designing pricing pages where anchoring, loss aversion, or social proof apply

## How to use

Invoke this skill by name (`marketing-psychology`) or with one of the trigger phrases in the frontmatter. The agent will apply behavioral science lenses — anchoring, scarcity, social proof, loss aversion, cognitive load — to the copy or design surface you provide.

Typical workflow:
1. Provide the copy block, screenshot, or design description.
2. Agent audits through each relevant principle and flags gaps.
3. Agent rewrites or annotates with specific, actionable improvements.

## Output

Annotated copy or design with principle labels, rationale, and revised versions. No lorem ipsum; every output uses real content from the provided material.
