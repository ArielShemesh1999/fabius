# competitive-ads-extractor

## What it does

Pulls ads from public ad libraries (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn), structures the data, and analyzes competitor messaging angles, creative formats, and offer framing — so you understand what's working in market before writing your own.

## When to use

- Building a new campaign and need a creative benchmark
- Running a brand audit against direct competitors
- Identifying whitespace in competitor ad creative

## How to use

Trigger with a competitor name, ad library URL, or one of the trigger phrases in the frontmatter. The agent will extract ad data, categorize by format and message, and surface the patterns with a structured teardown.
