## 1 · Landing page anatomy

Each section has ONE job. If a section can't name its job, cut it (`fabius-parcus`). The CTA repeats at every scroll-stop, but it's the SAME CTA (§7 of the playbook).

| Section | Its one job | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero** | promise the outcome + the one CTA, above the fold | feature-listing instead of promising |
| **Social proof bar** | borrow credibility in one glance | logos nobody recognizes |
| **Problem** | mirror their pain so they feel seen | talking about you, not them |
| **Solution / how** | the mechanism — *why* it works, in 3 beats | a feature dump |
| **Proof** | numbers · demo · named testimonial (§3) | adjectives standing in for evidence |
| **Objections / FAQ** | disarm the top 3 doubts (playbook §6) | dodging the real objection (price) |
| **Pricing** | make the choice obvious — anchor + recommend one | three tiers with no guidance |
| **Final CTA** | last call + risk-reverser | a summary instead of an action |

**Skeleton:**

```
[HERO]
  Headline:    [the bold, ultra-specific promise — 4-U test]
  Subhead:     [who it's for + the mechanism, one line]
  CTA button:  [verb + outcome: "Start free", "Book a demo"]
  Visual:      [product shot / 12s demo, not a stock photo]
[PROOF BAR]   Trusted by [logo] [logo] [logo]  ·  "[one-line quote]"
[PROBLEM]     Today you [pain]. And it costs you [the deeper cost].
[SOLUTION]    [Product] [mechanism]. Here's how:
              1. [step/benefit]  2. [step/benefit]  3. [step/benefit]
[PROOF]       [Number]. [Demo]. "[Testimonial]" — [Name, role, company]
[OBJECTIONS]  "[Doubt]?" → [rebuttal]   ×3
[PRICING]     [Anchor tier]  ·  ★ [recommended tier]  ·  [entry tier]
[FINAL CTA]   [Restate the one CTA] · [free / cancel anytime / no card]
```

RTL note: mirror the whole grid — hero text-align right, CTA at the right edge, logo-bar reads right-to-left, numerals stay LTR inside the line.

## 2 · Email sequences

One ask per send (playbook §4). Three sequences, each a subject formula + a body template.

### Welcome (fires on signup — set the relationship, drive the first value action)

```
Email 1 (now):     deliver the promised thing + ONE activation step
Email 2 (day 1):   the quick win — "do this one thing, see the value"
Email 3 (day 3):   social proof + the next step up
Email 4 (day 5):   the soft ask (upgrade / book / invite) + risk-reverser
```

Subject formulas: `Welcome — here's your [thing]` · `Your first [win] in 2 minutes` · `[Name], one thing before you start` · `How [persona] got [result] with [product]`

```
Subject: Your first [win] in 2 minutes
Hey [name],
You signed up to [outcome]. The fastest way there: [the one action].
→ [single link/button]
Reply if you get stuck — a human reads these.
[sign-off]
```

### Launch (time-boxed — build tension toward a deadline)

```
Announce (day -3):  "It's coming" — the promise + the date
Open (day 0):       it's live · the offer · the CTA
Proof (day 1):      early results / testimonials + CTA
Objection (day 2):  answer the #1 hesitation + CTA
Last call (day 3):  the deadline tonight + CTA (get out of the way)
```

Subject formulas: `It's live: [thing]` · `[Offer] ends [day]` · `Last call — [deadline] tonight` · `Why we built [thing]` · `[N] teams already [result]`

```
Subject: It's live: [thing]
[One-line what it is + who it's for.]
[The single most compelling proof or promise.]
[The offer + the deadline, plainly.]
→ [one CTA]
[sign-off]
P.S. [restate the deadline — the P.S. is read more than the body]
```

### Nurture (no deadline — earn trust, stay top-of-mind, sell softly)

```
Cadence:  one useful email / week. ~80% give, ~20% ask.
Give:     a tip, a teardown, a result, a story — useful even if they never buy.
Ask:      every 4th–5th send, one soft CTA tied to that week's value.
```

Subject formulas: `The [mistake] most [persona] make` · `How to [outcome] without [cost]` · `[Result] in [timeframe] — the exact steps` · `One thing about [topic]`

```
Subject: The [mistake] most [persona] make
[name],
[The insight — one specific, useful idea they can act on today.]
[One concrete example / number.]
[Optional soft tie: "This is exactly what [product] automates."]
[sign-off]
```

## 3 · Social hooks

The hook is the first line — it earns the second. Platform sets the rhythm; the promise stays specific.

### X / Twitter (punchy, one idea, the first line is the whole bet)

```
Hook patterns:
  "[Bold claim]. Here's why:"
  "I [did thing] for [time]. [N] lessons:"
  "Most [persona] get [topic] wrong. The fix:"
  "[Surprising number] [context]. A thread 🧵"
  "Stop [common action]. Do this instead:"
Body: one idea per line · short lines · end on a takeaway or CTA.
```

Example hooks: *"Your landing page has one job. Most have five."* · *"We cut churn 40% by deleting a feature. The story:"*

### LinkedIn (hook + line break + one story/idea, professional but human)

```
Line 1 (the scroll-stopper, then a blank line):
  "[Counterintuitive statement]."  /  "[Result] — and it surprised me."
Body:
  [the setup — concrete situation]
  [the turn — what changed / what you learned]
  [the lesson — one transferable takeaway]
Close: a question (drives comments) OR a soft CTA.
```

Example hook: *"We turned down a $50k deal last week. Best decision of the quarter. Here's why:"*

### Instagram (visual-first — caption supports the image; hook in first 8 words)

```
First line (before the "...more" fold): the hook — curiosity or promise.
Caption: short, scannable, one CTA ("link in bio", "save this").
Carousel: slide 1 = the hook, last slide = the CTA.
```

Example hook: *"Save this before your next launch 👇"* · *"The 3-word fix for a flat headline:"*

RTL note: Hebrew/Arabic social copy reads right-aligned; the hook's punch word lands at the right edge. Keep hashtags and @handles in their native LTR run.

## 4 · Cold outreach

Personal, short, one ask. The reader owes you nothing — earn the reply in four lines.

### The 4-line cold email

```
Line 1 (trigger):   [specific, recent, true reason you're reaching THEM]
                    "Saw [company] just [event] — congrats / noticed [thing]."
Line 2 (relevance): [the problem that creates, in their world, one line]
Line 3 (proof):     [you solve it + one proof] "We helped [similar co] [result]."
Line 4 (ask):       [the smallest yes] "Worth a 15-min look? Reply 'yes' and I'll send a time."
```

Rules: no "I hope this finds you well", no feature list, no attachment, no second ask. Subject = 2–4 words, lowercase, looks internal: `quick question` · `[their company] + [yours]` · `re: [their recent thing]`.

### Follow-up cadence (most replies come from follow-ups, not the first send)

```
Day 0:  the 4-line email.
Day 3:  one-line bump — "Floating this up — worth a look?"
Day 7:  new angle — a different proof point or a relevant resource (give, don't nag).
Day 12: the break-up — "I'll assume the timing's off — happy to circle back in Q[x]?"
        (break-ups often out-pull every prior send.)
```

Stop after the break-up. Four touches, then move on (`fabius-parcus` — don't burn goodwill).

## 5 · Launch frames

### One-week launch calendar

```
Mon  (T-3)  Teaser: "something's coming [day]" — email + social. Build the list.
Tue  (T-2)  The why: the story/problem behind it. Warm the audience.
Wed  (T-1)  Last warning + early-access for the waitlist.
Thu  (T-0)  LAUNCH: email + every social channel + the offer goes live.
Fri  (T+1)  Proof: first results / testimonials. Re-share.
Sat  (T+2)  Objection-buster: answer the #1 hesitation.
Sun  (T+3)  Last call: the deadline tonight. Then close it.
```

### Waitlist → launch sequence

```
On waitlist signup:  confirm + set expectation ("you'll be first, on [date]").
T-7 to T-1:          drip 1–2 value/teaser emails — keep them warm, don't go silent.
T-0 early access:    waitlist gets the offer hours before public. Reward the wait.
T-0 public:          open to all.
T+window:            close / raise price. The deadline is the conversion engine.
```

A waitlist with no nurture goes cold by launch day. One touch keeps it alive.

## 6 · The swipe file

Copy-paste starters. Fill the brackets, run them through the 4-U test (playbook §5), keep what survives.

### Headline formulas

```
How to [outcome] without [the dreaded cost]
[Number] [things] every [persona] should [do] before [event]
The [adjective] way to [outcome] — and why [old way] fails
[Outcome] in [specific timeframe]. No [objection] required.
Finally, [outcome] built for [specific who]
Stop [painful thing]. Start [desired thing].
You don't need [expensive thing] to [outcome]. You need [your thing].
What [aspirational group] know about [topic] that you don't
Turn [input] into [output] in [time]
The [number]-[unit] [method] that [outcome]
[Product]: the [category] that [the one difference]
```

### CTA phrasings (verb + outcome > generic "submit")

```
Start free            Get my [outcome]        Book a 15-min demo
Claim your [thing]    See it in action        Try it — no card
Get started in [time] Send me the [resource]  Reserve my spot
Show me how           Fix this now            Get the template
```

Pair high-friction CTAs with a risk-reverser on the same line: `Start free · cancel anytime` · `Book a demo · no commitment`.

### Proof-line patterns (replace an adjective with one of these)

```
[Exact number] [unit] — measured, [context]        →  "0.3s render, p95"
[N] [customers/teams/users] already [result]        →  "3,214 teams shipped faster"
"[Verbatim quote]" — [Name, role, company]
[Before metric] → [after metric] in [timeframe]     →  "2h → 15min/day"
Featured in / Backed by / Trusted by [credible name]
[Percentage] of users [achieved outcome]            →  "78% cut churn in 30 days"
[Guarantee]: [specific promise or your money back]
```

### Objection rebuttals (name the doubt, then disarm — playbook §6)

```
"Too expensive"   → "It costs less than [the pain it removes]. And [risk-reverser]."
"Too complex"     → "Live in [short time] — no [dev team / migration / training]."
"No time to set up" → "[N]-minute setup. We do the [hard part] for you."
"Will it work for me?" → "[Similar customer] thought so too — here's their [result]."
"Already using [X]" → "Most switchers came from [X] because [the one difference]."
"What if it doesn't work?" → "[Free trial / money-back] — you risk nothing but [tiny thing]."
"Not the right time" → "The cost of waiting is [the compounding pain]. Start small: [smallest yes]."
```

### Subject-line starters

```
quick question            [their company] + [yours]        re: [recent thing]
It's live: [thing]        [offer] ends [day]                Last call — [deadline]
Your first [win]          The [mistake] most [persona] make How [persona] got [result]
[Name], one thing         [N] teams already [result]        Before you [action]...
```

RTL note: every template above works in Hebrew/Arabic — mirror alignment, keep numerals and Latin brand/handle runs LTR inside the RTL line, and let the punch word land at the right edge.
