# GTM outreach — the sales.gtm capability

Outbound and inbound sales work under mercatus discipline: research a market, qualify who is
actually a fit, and draft outreach that a specific human would recognize as written for them.
The doctrine in one line: **research is aggressive, qualification is honest, sending is never
fabius's act.**

## 1 · ICP before anything

No outreach work starts without an Ideal Customer Profile stated in checkable terms: segment,
size, role, the pain fabius's user actually solves, and the disqualifiers. "Everyone who
might benefit" is not an ICP — it is the absence of one. The ICP is written down (archivum)
so every later qualification decision traces to it.

## 2 · Lead research — public, respectful, sourced

- Research runs on **public sources the harness can legitimately reach**, under machina's
  fetch discipline — robots and terms-of-service are a fail-closed gate, never an obstacle to
  route around. No scraping where scraping is prohibited; no deanonymization of private
  individuals; no purchased-list laundering.
- Every fact recorded about a prospect carries its source. A prospect file with unsourced
  claims poisons the personalization built on it.

## 3 · Qualification — a score with stated criteria

Each lead is scored against the ICP's written criteria — fit, evidence of the pain, timing
signal — and the score's *why* is stated per lead. Disqualifying is a first-class outcome:
a lean pipeline of real fits beats a long list that flatters the dashboard. Won and lost
deals feed back (the user's own data only): what the wins had in common tightens the ICP;
what the losses said becomes the objection list the next draft answers.

## 4 · Outreach drafts — personalization must be true

- A draft opens with a **real, verifiable observation** about the prospect — something the
  research actually found, with its source in the prospect file. Fake familiarity ("loved
  your recent post" with no post) is a lie in the first line, and it reads like one.
- One draft, one ask, one reader. Mercatus rules apply: proof over adjectives,
  awareness-match (a cold reader gets problem-language, not product-language), one action.
- **Draft ≠ send.** Outreach lands on the DRAFT rung of the acting ladder; the send is the
  user's act, through their own tools, under their own name. fabius never operates a sending
  channel on its own authority.
- **The law is a floor, not a nuisance:** unsolicited-message law (Israel's spam amendment,
  and the local equivalent wherever the reader sits) governs consent, sender identification,
  and opt-out. A cadence is a few spaced, escalating-value touches with a clean stop on any
  negative signal — persistence past a "no" is not persistence, it is spam.

## 5 · The seams

Meeting booked → the brief and the record are archivum's meeting-capture job. The deck the
meeting needs → decor's presentation doctrine, mercatus leading the narrative. A recurring
outreach *pipeline* (trigger → research → draft → human review) wired across the user's real
tools → machina builds it, with the same draft-only boundary; any writes the user's own
pipeline makes to their CRM stay typed and sourced. Positioning, the funnel, and the copy voice stay in the mercatus playbook — this
file only adds the outbound lane.
