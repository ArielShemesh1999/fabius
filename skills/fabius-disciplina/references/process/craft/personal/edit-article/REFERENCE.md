---
name: fabius-disciplina-edit-article
description: Edit and improve articles by restructuring sections, improving clarity, and tightening prose. Use when user wants to edit, revise, or improve an article draft.
---

1. Divide the article into sections by heading. For each section, identify its core point.

Treat information as a directed acyclic graph — each piece may depend on others. Order sections so dependencies are satisfied before they are needed.

Confirm the section plan with the user.

2. For each section, rewrite to improve clarity, coherence, and flow. Keep paragraphs to 240 characters maximum.
