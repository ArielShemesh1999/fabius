Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach shared understanding. Walk each branch of the decision tree in dependency order — resolve blockers before moving on. For each question, give your recommended answer before asking mine.

Ask one question at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore it first instead of asking.
